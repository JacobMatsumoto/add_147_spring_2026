About Loretta

As a freelance insurance agent, Loretta Matsumoto is committed to helping people feel confident and supported when making important coverage decisions. Licensed in Illinois, Michigan, Texas, Kansas, Ohio, South Dakota, South Carolina, and New Mexico, she offers a full suite of services including Life, Health, Property & Casualty, Limited Lines, LTCP, and Motor Vehicle insurance. Loretta prides herself on being approachable and easy to work with. She strives to be someone clients can trust to explain their options, answer questions, and provide dependable guidance every step of the way.
